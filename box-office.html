A quick note about the numbers

Figures are based on TMDB movie records and can be missing or updated over time. Worldwide gross is not profit, and the reported budget does not include every production, marketing, or distribution cost. “Bollywood in India” is filtered to Hindi-original-language titles currently listed for Indian theatres; revenue shown on those cards is still worldwide revenue where reported, not verified India-only collections.

Movie metadata and images provided by The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
