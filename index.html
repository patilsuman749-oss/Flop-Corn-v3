Add Flop Corn to your Home Screen

Flop Corn isn't a downloadable .apk on iPhone — install it as an app straight from Safari instead:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add in the top right corner

Flop Corn will then open full-screen from your Home Screen, just like a regular app.
